# Streaming & Video

<p class="bl-page-lead">100% legitimate free movies, documentaries, public film archives, discovery trackers, and open-source media players.</p>

## Free Legal Streaming

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Kanopy](https://www.kanopy.com/)** — Ad-free indie films, Criterion classics, and documentaries free with a participating public library or university card. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Tubi](https://tubitv.com/)** — Huge catalog of licensed movies, TV series, and cult classics streamed free with light ads. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Pluto TV](https://pluto.tv/)** — Free live channels and on-demand movies and shows with no account required. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Plex Free Movies & TV](https://watch.plex.tv/)** — Free ad-supported movies, documentaries, and live channels accessible worldwide. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[PBS Video](https://www.pbs.org/video/)** — High-quality documentaries, NOVA science specials, and Frontline investigative journalism.

## Public Archives & Documentaries

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Internet Archive Moving Images](https://archive.org/details/movies)** — Millions of public-domain films, historic broadcasts, newsreels, and independent shorts. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[National Film Board of Canada (NFB)](https://www.nfb.ca/)** — Thousands of award-winning documentaries and animated short films free to watch globally.
- **[PeerTube / Sepia Search](https://sepiasearch.org/)** — Decentralized, ad-free federated video search across open PeerTube instances. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Open Culture Free Movies](https://www.openculture.com/freemoviesonline)** — Curated index of 4,000+ classic, noir, silent, and international public-domain films.

## Media Players & Home Servers

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[VLC Media Player](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/)** — Legendary open-source player that plays virtually every video and audio codec out of the box. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[mpv](https://mpv.io/)** — Minimalist, scriptable, hardware-accelerated open-source media player loved by power users. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/)** — 100% free and open-source personal media server to stream your own library to any device. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge> <TagBadge type="privacy">Privacy</TagBadge>
- **[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)** — Feature-rich open-source CLI tool for archiving public-domain and Creative Commons web videos. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>

## Tracking & Discovery

- **[Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/)** — Social film diary, curated lists, and community ratings for cinephiles. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/)** — Search engine showing exactly where any movie or show is legally streaming in your country. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[SeriesGraph](https://seriesgraph.com/)** — Visual episode-by-episode rating heatmaps for TV shows. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
