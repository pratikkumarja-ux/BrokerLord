# Reading & Books

<p class="bl-page-lead">Beautifully typeset public-domain classics, open-access academic research, free audiobooks, and reading tools.</p>

## Public-Domain Ebooks

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/)** — Volunteer-driven project producing meticulously proofread, gorgeously typeset public-domain ebooks. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge> <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/)** — Over 70,000 free public-domain ebooks in EPUB, Kindle, and plain-text formats. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/)** — Free online library of historical documents, poems, translations, and reference texts.
- **[Global Grey Ebooks](https://www.globalgreyebooks.com/)** — Hand-formatted free classics focusing on philosophy, mythology, history, and esoteric literature.

## Digital Libraries & Open-Access Research

- **[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/)** — Internet Archive's open, editable library catalog with controlled digital lending and millions of readable books.
- **[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/)** — Open-access preprint archive for physics, mathematics, computer science, AI, and quantitative finance. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)](https://doaj.org/)** — Independent index of 20,000+ peer-reviewed, free-to-read scientific journals.
- **[PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)** — Free full-text archive of biomedical and life sciences journal literature.
- **[Libby](https://www.overdrive.com/apps/libby)** — Borrow current ebooks, magazines, and audiobooks free using your local public library card.

## Free Audiobooks

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[LibriVox](https://librivox.org/)** — Free public-domain audiobooks read by volunteers from around the world. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Loyal Books](http://www.loyalbooks.com/)** — Browse and stream classic public-domain audiobooks and companion ebooks cleanly.

## Ebook Managers & RSS Readers

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/)** — Powerful open-source ebook library manager, format converter, and e-reader sync suite. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[KOReader](https://koreader.rocks/)** — Open-source document viewer for e-ink devices (Kobo, Kindle, PocketBook) and Android with advanced PDF reflow. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Foliate](https://johnfactotum.github.io/foliate/)** — Clean, distraction-free open-source EPUB reader for Linux desktops. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Miniflux](https://miniflux.app/)** — Minimalist, fast, open-source self-hosted RSS & Atom feed reader. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
