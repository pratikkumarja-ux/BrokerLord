# Miscellaneous

<p class="bl-page-lead">Open maps, free design assets, ad-free recipes, live weather & flight radars, and delightful corners of the internet.</p>

## Maps, Weather & Live Tracking

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)** — The free, editable, community-built map of the entire world powering thousands of open apps. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge> <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Windy](https://www.windy.com/)** — Gorgeous interactive global weather radar showing wind currents, storms, air quality, and satellite layers. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/)** — Unfiltered, community-fed real-time global aircraft tracking map. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Earth Nullschool](https://earth.nullschool.net/)** — Real-time animated visualization of global wind, ocean currents, and atmospheric conditions.

## Free Design Assets, Icons & Fonts

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/)** — Crisp, consistent, open-source icon library with 1,500+ customizable SVGs. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/)** — High-quality, free-for-commercial-use typefaces curated by the Indian Type Foundry. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/)** — Millions of high-resolution, free-to-use photographs from global photographers.
- **[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)** — Over 100 million freely usable media files, historical photos, maps, and diagrams.

## Cooking, Health & Everyday Utilities

- **[Based.Cooking](https://based.cooking/)** — Ultra-fast, zero-bloat, zero-ad open-source recipe site with straight-to-the-point ingredients and steps. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/)** — Multi-modal global travel planner comparing trains, buses, ferries, and flights between any two points.
- **[iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/)** — Free, step-by-step repair manuals for phones, laptops, consoles, and everyday electronics. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>

## Fun & Curiosity Sites

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Neal.fun](https://neal.fun/)** — Inventive collection of interactive web toys (*The Deep Sea, Infinite Craft, Size of Space, Password Game*). <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[WindowSwap](https://www.window-swap.com/)** — Peek out of real windows filmed by people in cities across the globe.
- **[Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/)** — Curated world map of hidden wonders, unusual museums, and offbeat landmarks.
- **[Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/)** — Explore over 900 billion archived web pages spanning three decades of internet history.
