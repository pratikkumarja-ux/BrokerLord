# Gaming

<p class="bl-page-lead">Play great free & open-source games, browse indie treasures, track verified giveaways, and preserve gaming history.</p>

## Free & Open-Source Games

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[itch.io Free Games](https://itch.io/games/free)** — Massive catalog of creative indie games, jam winners, and experimental art games. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[0 A.D.](https://play0ad.com/)** — Gorgeously crafted, free and open-source historical real-time strategy game of ancient warfare. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[OpenTTD](https://www.openttd.org/)** — Open-source transport simulation classic with multiplayer, mods, and active updates. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Mindustry](https://mindustrygame.github.io/)** — Addictive open-source factory-building and tower-defense hybrid for desktop and mobile. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[OpenRA](https://www.openra.net/)** — Modern open-source engine reimagining classic real-time strategy titles with online play. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Lichess](https://lichess.org/)** — 100% free, ad-free, open-source online chess server with built-in puzzles and Stockfish analysis. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge> <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>

## Launchers & Preservation Frameworks

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Playnite](https://playnite.link/)** — Open-source unified game library manager combining Steam, GOG, Epic, itch.io, and emulators. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Heroic Games Launcher](https://heroicgameslauncher.com/)** — Open-source launcher for Epic Games, GOG, and Amazon Prime Gaming on Linux, macOS, and Windows. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[RetroArch](https://www.retroarch.com/)** — Open-source frontend for game preservation cores, shaders, netplay, and retro controllers. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Flashpoint Archive](https://flashpointarchive.org/)** — Webgame preservation project archiving 170,000+ historic Flash, Shockwave, and HTML5 browser games.
- **[Emulation General Wiki](https://emulation.gametechwiki.com/)** — Comprehensive technical encyclopedia on hardware emulation and preservation projects.

## Deal Trackers, Databases & Discovery

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[IsThereAnyDeal](https://isthereanydeal.com/)** — Price history tracker and alert service covering legitimate PC game storefronts and free giveaways. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/)** — Unofficial real-time database tracking Steam player counts, lowest historical prices, and free promotions. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[ProtonDB](https://www.protondb.com/)** — Community compatibility reports for running PC games on Linux and Steam Deck.
- **[PCGamingWiki](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/)** — Essential fixes, ultrawide patches, FOV tweaks, and config paths for thousands of PC games.
- **[Backloggd](https://www.backloggd.com/)** — Clean game collection tracker, diary, and community review platform.
