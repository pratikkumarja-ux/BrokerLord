# Educational

<p class="bl-page-lead">World-class university lectures, interactive coding curricula, visual math tools, and open textbooks — free for everyone.</p>

## University Courses & Open Textbooks

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)** — Full lecture videos, problem sets, exams, and syllabi from over 2,500 MIT courses. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[CS50 (Harvard)](https://cs50.harvard.edu/)** — Harvard's world-famous free introductory courses in Computer Science, Python, Web, AI, and Cybersecurity.
- **[OpenStax](https://openstax.org/)** — Peer-reviewed, openly licensed college and high-school textbooks from Rice University in PDF and web formats. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Class Central](https://www.classcentral.com/)** — Search engine aggregating thousands of free university courses across MIT, Stanford, edX, and Coursera.

## Coding & Computer Science

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/)** — Interactive, project-based full-stack web dev, Python, data science, and algorithm certifications — 100% free. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/)** — Community-created visual learning paths, skill trees, and guides for modern software engineering roles. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/)** — Rigorous, open-source full-stack JavaScript and Ruby on Rails curriculum built around real projects. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[OSSU Computer Science](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science)** — Complete, self-taught undergraduate computer science degree path built strictly from free online courses. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/)** — MIT course teaching shells, Git, Vim, debugging, profiling, and command-line fluency.

## Math, Science & Interactive Visuals

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)** — Non-profit mastery learning across math, physics, economics, computing, and history for all ages.
- **[3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com/)** — Animated visual intuition for linear algebra, calculus, neural networks, and higher mathematics.
- **[Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator)** — Blazing-fast free graphing, scientific, and 3D calculator right in the browser. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[PhET Interactive Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu/)** — University of Colorado interactive labs for physics, chemistry, biology, and earth science.
- **[WolframAlpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com/)** — Computational knowledge engine for symbolic math, step checks, physics, and curated datasets.

## Spaced Repetition & Languages

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)** — Open-source spaced-repetition flashcard program powered by FSRS scheduling for long-term retention. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Language Transfer](https://www.languagetransfer.org/)** — Completely free, audio-based structural language courses (Spanish, German, Greek, Arabic, French, and more).
