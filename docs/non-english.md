# Non-English

<p class="bl-page-lead">Multilingual digital libraries, international public broadcasters, open translation tools, and cultural archives from around the world.</p>

## Multilingual Libraries & Open Archives

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/)** — Access millions of digitized books, artworks, maps, films, and recordings from European museums and libraries in 20+ languages. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[Gallica (BnF)](https://gallica.bnf.fr/)** — Digital library of the National Library of France offering millions of free French books, manuscripts, and periodicals.
- **[Aozora Bunko](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/)** — Japan's largest open digital library of public-domain Japanese literature and classic novels.
- **[National Digital Library of India (NDLI)](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in/)** — Massive multilingual academic & cultural repository spanning Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, English, and more.
- **[Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/)** — Rich open collection of classic Spanish and Latin American literature and historical texts.

## International Public Media & Documentaries

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[ARTE.tv](https://www.arte.tv/)** — Acclaimed European public-service cultural channel offering free documentaries, concerts, and cinema in French, German, English, Spanish, Polish, and Italian. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[NHK World-Japan](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/)** — Free live broadcasts, Japanese culture documentaries, and language lessons in 18+ languages.
- **[Deutsche Welle (DW)](https://www.dw.com/)** — Global news, documentaries, and structured free German courses (*Nicos Weg*) in 30 languages.

## Open Translation & Dictionary Tools

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Wiktionary](https://www.wiktionary.org/)** — Collaborative multilingual dictionary with etymologies, IPA pronunciations, and declension tables across hundreds of languages. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/)** — Huge open database of real-world example sentences translated across 400+ languages with native audio. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[Jisho.org](https://jisho.org/)** — Fast, powerful Japanese-English dictionary with radical, kanji stroke, and example sentence lookup. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[LibreTranslate](https://libretranslate.com/)** — Open-source, self-hostable machine translation engine powered by Argos Translate. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
