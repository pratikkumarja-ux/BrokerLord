# Listening & Audio

<p class="bl-page-lead">Discover global radio stations, independent music, royalty-free sound libraries, and studio-grade open-source audio software.</p>

## Internet Radio & Ambient

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Radio Garden](https://radio.garden/)** — Spin an interactive 3D globe to tune into thousands of live local radio stations around the world. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[SomaFM](https://somafm.com/)** — Listener-supported, commercial-free underground electronic, ambient, jazz, and focus radio channels. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[NTS Radio](https://www.nts.live/)** — Global independent online radio featuring genre-spanning live sets and infinite mixtapes.
- **[Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/)** — Community-maintained open database of 40,000+ free internet radio streams. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[MyNoise](https://mynoise.net/)** — Interactive calibrated background noise generators for deep focus, sleep, and relaxation.

## Free Music & Royalty-Free Audio

- **[Internet Archive Live Music](https://archive.org/details/etree)** — Hundreds of thousands of artist-approved live concert recordings free to stream and download.
- **[Free Music Archive (FMA)](https://freemusicarchive.org/)** — Curated Creative Commons music library for listening and creator projects.
- **[Freesound](https://freesound.org/)** — Collaborative database of 600,000+ Creative Commons sound effects, field recordings, and samples. <TagBadge type="web">Web</TagBadge>
- **[ccMixter](http://ccmixter.org/)** — Community music site featuring remixes, samples, and a cappellas under Creative Commons licenses.

## Audio Editing, Players & Music Creation

- <span class="bl-star">★ Top Pick</span> **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/)** — Classic free, open-source multi-track audio editor and recorder. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[LMMS](https://lmms.io/)** — Free, cross-platform open-source digital audio workstation for beats, synths, and melodies. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[MuseScore](https://musescore.org/)** — Professional-grade open-source music notation and sheet music composition software. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
- **[foobar2000](https://www.foobar2000.org/)** — Lightweight, endlessly customizable audiophile desktop music player.
- **[MusicBrainz Picard](https://picard.musicbrainz.org/)** — Open-source audio tagger using acoustic fingerprinting to organize local music collections. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>

## Music Discovery & Tracking

- **[RateYourMusic (RYM)](https://rateyourmusic.com/)** — Deep genre charts, community reviews, and music discovery database.
- **[ListenBrainz](https://listenbrainz.org/)** — Open-source listening history tracker and recommendation engine from MetaBrainz. <TagBadge type="foss">FOSS</TagBadge>
