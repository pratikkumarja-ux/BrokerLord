# November 2026 Updates 🍂

*Posted November 27, 2026*

Welcome to the launch update of **BrokerLord**! This release establishes the core directory architecture, theming engine, and initial set of vetted resources across 13 major categories.

## Site & UI Highlights

- **Dark & AMOLED Mode Toggle:** Switch effortlessly between **Light**, **Dark**, and true pitch-black **AMOLED (`#000000`)** directly from the navbar, persisted across reloads with zero flash.
- **Live Accent Palette Picker:** Choose from 7 FMHY-inspired accent themes (*Color Swarm Indigo, Nebula Violet, Emerald, Cyber Cyan, Crimson Rose, Solar Amber, Monochrome*).
- **Instant Local Search:** Press `/` or `Ctrl + K` to search every tool, description, and heading client-side with fuzzy matching and detailed previews.
- **Category Filter Grid:** Filter the homepage cards by *Privacy, AI & Web*, *Media & Reading*, or *Apps & OS*.
